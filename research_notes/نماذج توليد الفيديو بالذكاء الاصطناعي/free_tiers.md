# Free Ways to Generate AI Video (as of 25 Nov 2026)

Method note: research ran on 25 Nov 2026 using WebSearch only. WebFetch could not reach sand.ai, support.google.com or dreamina.capcut.com because they are not on this session's network allowlist, so I could not open any official pricing page directly. Almost every number below comes from search snippets of third-party pricing blogs, and those blogs often disagree. Treat each figure as "reported", not verified, unless it is marked as coming from an official page. Free-tier terms in this market change month to month.

## 1. Consumer free tiers and daily/monthly credits (by product)

### Takeaway
Few free routes recur and are still useful in late Nov 2026. In rough order of value: Google Flow (reportedly 50 credits a day, about 5 Veo 3.1 Lite clips a day), Kling (66 credits a day, about 2 short 720p watermarked clips), Dreamina/CapCut (about 120 credits a day in the US, region-dependent), PixVerse (30–60 credits a day, low resolution, watermarked), Bing Video Creator (Sora 2, 10 fast generations then unlimited slow), and Sand.ai MAGI (a small monthly free allowance). Several famous free routes are gone or close to gone: the Sora app (reportedly discontinued), Grok Imagine video (paywalled, with sporadic 1/day), Gemini app video for free accounts, Higgsfield (trial only), Runway (one-time credits only), and Pika (0 monthly credits).

- One source says Google retires Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni Flash 1.1 inside Flow on 5 Dec 2026. — [saascrmreview – Flow pricing](https://saascrmreview.com/google-flow-pricing/) (unverified)
- Gemini app: the most recent reports (about Nov 2026) say video in the Gemini app now runs on "Gemini Omni", which replaced Veo, and needs a paid Google AI plan. ## 2. Free API credits for developers

### Takeaway
There is essentially no meaningful free video API in late 2026. Google's Gemini API gives free-tier Veo access of 0 to a handful of requests at most. fal.ai's free credits work only in its web playground. WaveSpeed gives $1 of trial credit, which is less than one premium clip. Kie.ai and Replicate offer small, unspecified trials.

### Cited Findings
- Gemini API / AI Studio: forum moderators say "Veo API access is very limited for free tier". One free user saw Veo 3 at a 0/2 limit, and a staff reply said AI Studio "doesn't guarantee video generation". Paid Tier 1 Veo 3 Fast was cited at 10 requests a day. — [discuss.ai.google.dev](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/gemini-api-for-veo3-for-students-free-account/101147); [discuss.ai.google.dev](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/gemini-api-veo-3/97560)
- One source says the Veo 3.1 Preview models in the Gemini API shut down on 22 Oct 2026, with developers pointed to Gemini Omni Flash or the GA Veo models, and that there is no free tier for video. — [cloudzero](https://www.cloudzero.com/blog/veo-3-pricing/) / [aibusinessweekly](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/google-gemini-video) (unverified)
- fal.ai: free credits and coupons are "only usable in Sandbox and the Playground — they cannot be used through the API". New accounts are limited to 2 concurrent requests. fal reportedly removes its Veo endpoints on 17 Nov 2026. — [teamday.ai (Nov 2026)](https://www.teamday.ai/blog/ai-image-video-api-providers-comparison-2026); [seedrouter.ai](https://seedrouter.ai/blog/fal-ai-alternatives)
- WaveSpeedAI: $1 trial credit for eligible new accounts, with some premium models excluded and no card needed. This is from WaveSpeed's own blog, so it is self-interested. — [wavespeed.ai](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/ai-api-pricing/ai-credits-expire-policies-compared/)
- Replicate: no fixed signup amount. Some models can be run free "for a while". — [teamday.ai](https://www.teamday.ai/blog/ai-image-video-api-providers-comparison-2026)
- Kie.ai: free trial credits (amount not published) plus a free Playground, and credits reportedly never expire. — [kie.ai market](https://kie.ai/market/text-to-video); [bitdoze review](https://www.bitdoze.com/kie-ai-video-generation/)
- OpenAI Sora 2 API reportedly shut down on 24 Sep 2026. — [teamday.ai](https://www.teamday.ai/blog/ai-image-video-api-providers-comparison-2026)

### Inferences
- Running open-weight models (Wan 2.x via Hugging Face Spaces or ZeroGPU, or locally) is the only truly free developer route, and it is rate-limited and needs GPU access.

### Gaps
- Exact Kie.ai trial amount, and whether Replicate's free runs cover any video model.

## 3. Availability in Egypt / Middle East

### Takeaway
Google is the strongest free route for Egypt. Flow officially lists Egypt, and Egyptian university students can claim a free year of Google AI Plus (which includes Veo/Flow credits) until 31 Dec 2026. Meta's Vibes was not officially launched in the region. For Kling, Hailuo, PixVerse and Dreamina I found no official region list either way, and they are generally described as globally available.

### Cited Findings
- Egypt is on Google's official "Where you can use Flow" country list. VPNs do not enable access, and users must be 18+. — [Google Flow Help](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353544); [Labs help](https://support.google.com/labs/answer/16353544)
- Egyptian student offer (MCIT and Google): one free year of Google AI Plus for students aged 18+, with registration open until 31 Dec 2026. It reportedly includes Veo video generation and 400 GB of storage, plus up to 75% off AI Pro. — [Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/1/578797/Egypt/Egypt,-Google-offer-university-students-one-year-o.aspx); [felloai](https://felloai.com/how-to-claim-your-free-google-ai-pro-for-students/)
- The earlier 2025 offer (free 12-month AI Pro for students in Egypt and Saudi Arabia, register before 3 Nov 2025) has closed. — [Google MENA blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/company-news/outreach-initiatives/free-gemini-pro-students-saudi-arabia-egypt/); [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/google-gemini-pro-free-students-egypt-saudi/)
- Meta Vibes was not officially available in the UAE or Egypt at launch. Meta AI launched in Egypt in Feb 2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/facebook-instagram-owner-meta-launch-vibes-an-ai-video-feed-1.500284282); [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/meta-ai-launches-in-the-middle-east-empowering-new-era-of-creativity-and-connection)
- Kling: Kuaishou announced global availability (date unclear). I found no Egypt-specific restriction. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2814856)
- Dreamina's daily credits vary by region; the 120/day figure is for the US. — [seedance.tv](https://www.seedance.tv/dreamina)
- Jimeng is China-focused (Douyin login). — [comparebestai](https://comparebestai.com/tools/jimeng-ai)

### Inferences
- Before the 31 Dec 2026 deadline, Egyptian university students should prioritize the AI Plus offer. It adds 200 Flow credits a month on top of the 50 a day, if the Flow figures above are right.
- Users should not use VPNs or fake regions to bypass restrictions. Google states that VPNs will not enable access, and doing so risks the account.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation of Kling, Hailuo, PixVerse, Vidu, Qwen Chat or Dreamina availability or daily credit amounts specifically for Egyptian accounts. These need hands-on testing from Egypt.
- Whether Bing Video Creator and Gemini Omni video are offered in Egypt.

## 4. Free-tier limits: watermark, resolution, queue, commercial rights; scams and abuse

### Takeaway
Almost every free tier means a visible watermark, 720p or lower, slow or peak-hour-blocked queues, and personal or non-commercial use only. Exceptions are reported but not verified (Flow, with only an invisible SynthID mark). Multi-account farming, "unlimited free" resellers and "free Sora/Veo" lookalike domains should be avoided.

### Cited Findings
- Kling free: 720p, visible watermark, non-commercial. — [crixpix](https://crixpix.com/kling-ai-free/)
- PixVerse free: watermark, up to about 540p, no commercial license. — [magichour](https://magichour.ai/blog/pixverse-pricing)
- Vidu free: watermark, personal and non-commercial use. — [therundown](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/vidu)
- Dreamina free downloads are watermarked. — [hokai.io](https://hokai.io/guides/seedance-2-free-where-to-use-2026)
- Runway free is watermarked. Luma free is reportedly watermarked and non-commercial. — [whichoneisreal](https://whichoneisreal.com/compare/best-free-ai-video/); [aitoolpick](https://aitoolpick.org/blog/luma-ai-pricing-2026/)
- Flow free can block video generation at peak hours (about 2–5 PM UTC, which is about 4–7 PM Cairo time). Bing has 10 fast generations, then a slow queue. — [saascrmreview](https://saascrmreview.com/google-flow-free/); [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/microsoft-upgrades-bing-video-creator-with-openais-sora-2-ai-video-model/)
- Scam and lookalike signals: many domains imitate the real products (for example "veo3ai.io", "grokimagine15.ai", "qwen3lm.com", "try-qwen-ai.com", "seedance.tv"). These are unofficial third-party sites, and several make unverified "free for everyone" claims. — e.g. [veo3ai.io](https://www.veo3ai.io/blog/veo-3-1-free-for-everyone-how-to-use-2026); [qwen3lm.com](https://qwen3lm.com/video-generator/)
- The Higgsfield trial reportedly needs a card and auto-converts to a paid plan. — [layer3labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/is-higgsfield-ai-free)
- Unofficial reverse-engineered "free API" wrappers (for example a Jimeng free-api Docker image) exist. Using them likely violates the terms of service. — [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/vinlic/jimeng-free-api/tags)

### Inferences
- Only official domains should be recommended: klingai.com, hailuoai.video, pixverse.ai, vidu.com, dreamina.capcut.com, labs.google/flow, chat.qwen.ai and the Bing app. Do not recommend multi-account farming, temp-mail signups, VPN region-hopping or ToS-violating wrapper APIs.
- Commercial use (for example in client or YouTube monetized work) generally requires a paid plan on Kling, PixVerse, Vidu and Luma.

### Gaps
- No verified data on Flow free-tier resolution (720p vs 1080p) or a visible watermark, or on Qwen Chat output resolution or watermark and commercial terms.
